4. Dataset Feature Categories

The dataset is very well-structured and scientifically designed, grouping variables into distinct physics-based categories:

Growth Dynamics

Tracks perimeter expansion rates and fuel consumption. Tells the model how fast the hazard is ballooning.

radial_growth growth_rate log1p_growth

Proximity Metrics

Evaluates distance contraction relative to safe civil assets. This is the single strongest threat signal.

dist_min_ci dist_slope_ci log_dist_min

Kinematics

Maps spatial coordinate shifts and core displacement vectors of the fire's advancing center of mass.

centroid_speed displacement closing_speed

Alignment Flow

Resolves spread angles to check whether the fire is heading directly toward surrounding evacuation zones.

alignment_abs bearing_sin bearing_cos

Time Density

Tracks perimeter snapshot counts, observation duration windows, and seasonal monthly indices.

num_perimeters dt_first_last start_month
8. Feature Engineering Quality

The feature engineering is one of the strongest components of this project. The project created highly meaningful, physically-derived features such as Closing Speed, Alignment Cosine, Distance Acceleration, Projected Advance Distance, and Relative Growth Rates.

These are high-value engineered features that reflect the physical dynamics of fire. In machine learning competitions and real-world industrial projects, good features often matter much more than simply running highly complex models on raw, unrefined fields.

Wildfire Spatio-Temporal Spread Dynamics & Kinematics

The vector equations map centroid displacement over time, resolving wind vectors and fire momentum into **Along-Track** (direct hazard breach) and **Cross-Track** (sideways spread) speeds.
