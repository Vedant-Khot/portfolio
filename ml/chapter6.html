20. Strengths of the Project
  • Strong Problem Selection: Addresses a highly critical, real-world challenge in climate and disaster management.
  • Advanced ML Concepts: Moves beyond simple classification, adapting complex survival analysis and model ensembles.
  • Excellent Feature Engineering: Derives physically meaningful spatial variables that reflect fire physics.
  • Real-World Social Impact: Directly aligns with global sustainability goals and community safety.
  • Thorough EDA: Uncovers actionable insights, such as July–August seasonality and starting distance thresholds.
  • Rigorous Evaluation: Uses appropriate metrics (C-Index) and advanced ensembling techniques.
  • Interdisciplinary Approach: Blends computer science, climate physics, and geographical information systems.
21. Weaknesses & Areas for Improvement
  • Small Dataset Volume: Sourcing only 221 events introduces overfitting risks and limits model generalization.
  • Missing GNN Details: Lacks specific details on GNN architectures, coordinate graph building, and comparative results.
  • No Real-Time Pipeline: The system operates offline without live satellite, telemetry, or weather streams.
  • Limited Model Explainability: Lacks interpretability tools like SHAP or LIME to explain complex predictions.
22. Future Improvements

Real-Time GIS & Weather Fusing

Active R&D

Integrating live NOAA and regional weather telemetry streams. Dynamic wind grids and fuel dryness levels will continuously adjust the fire spread direction and closing speed vector coefficients.

Multi-Spectral Satellite Ingestion

Phase 2

Fusing Sentinel-2 and Landsat active infrared bands to segments fire boundaries, automatically updating the initial perimeter coordinates.

Topographical Elevation Modeling

Planned

Loading high-resolution Digital Elevation Models (DEM) to incorporate fuel slope vectors and local convective thermal drafts that accelerate uphill flame progression.

Autonomous UAVs & Rescue Dashboard

Future Scope

Deploying autonomous drone coordinate tracking fleets coupled with a web-based real-time GIS warning dashboard, assisting fire departments in planning safer escape lanes.
