Why Use 4 Models? — Each Plays a Unique Role

What is ensemble?
AFT MODEL

XGBoost AFT

⏱ Predicts: Exact Time-to-Breach

The Accelerated Failure Time model gives a direct estimate of how many hours until breach. Unlike Cox, it outputs a concrete time, making it valuable for ETA calculation in operational alerts.

COX MODEL

XGBoost Cox

📊 Predicts: Relative Hazard Ranking

Cox Proportional Hazards computes a risk ranking score for each fire. It's best at distinguishing which fires are most dangerous relative to each other — critical for triage and priority ordering.

Random Survival Forest

🌲 Captures: Non-Linear Interactions

Tree-based survival forest captures complex non-linear patterns that parametric models miss — like sudden threshold effects where fires accelerate dramatically once crossing a certain distance.

Instead of averaging raw probabilities, we blend the hazard rank order from each model. This technique preserves ordinal safety relationships and shields the final prediction from individual model outliers.

Graph Neural Network Extension

Beyond the 4 tabular models, the pipeline also includes a Graph Neural Network (GNN) that constructs k-NN spatial graphs between grid cells. This traces how heat and smoke propagate through neighboring geographic vertices — capturing the connectivity of fire spread that tabular models miss. It places this project at the frontier of Spatial AI and environmental disaster intelligence.
