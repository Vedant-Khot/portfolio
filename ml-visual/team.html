Research Team

The Wildfire Evacuation Threat Prediction case study is an interdisciplinary research project developing cutting-edge machine learning architectures and survival analysis formulations to secure communities against escalating climate-induced disasters. Under the mentorship of Prof. Sneha Varur, the team engineered a robust spatio-temporal early warning system designed to safeguard civil infrastructure and human lives.
