View Methodology: Research Chronicle Overview

This modeling chronicle logs the entire research evolution of the **WiDS Datathon 2026** wildfire risk forecasting system. The timeline captures the progression from classical baseline statistical models to physics-informed feature spaces, multi-brain architectures, synthetic digital twins, and ultimately empirical calibration expert systems.

Phase 4: Physics Ensemble with Digital Twins

V11 Physics Ensemble

The first pipeline iteration combining all domain physics discoveries into a single rigorous cross-validated framework.

Model Strategy Score (Out-of-Fold)
v11_physics_ensemble.py 5-fold CatBoost Two-Brain + 15x Digital Twins + Isotonic Calibration + TTI Gate 0.9695 OOF Hybrid
Core Pipeline Innovations:
  • Independent-Jitter Digital Twins: Generated 1,200 synthetic fires by adding randomized noise to historical coordinate vectors, stabilizing validation.
  • TTI Physics Gate: Hard-coded constraint filter silencing threats that are physically incapable of reaching the zone within 72h based on maximum spread speed.
  • Per-Horizon Isotonic Calibration: Aligning raw model output probability ranks back to actual observed spatial breach frequencies.
